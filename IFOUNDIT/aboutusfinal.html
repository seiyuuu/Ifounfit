About IFoundIT

Get to know the platform, our mission, and the history behind Laguna College.

IFoundIT is a web-based lost and found system made for Laguna College. It gives students, faculty, and staff one place to report lost items, browse found items, and submit claims, so belongings get back to their owners faster and more easily.

We built IFoundIT as a team because lost items on campus are easy to forget when there is no single place to look. We designed every page ourselves, from the home feed and report form to the login, user dashboard, and admin dashboard, using HTML, CSS, and Javascript.

Every report is reviewed by an administrator before it goes live, so belongings get back to their rightful owners faster and more safely.

Our Mission

To make recovering lost belongings simple, organized, and stress-free for the whole campus.

Our Vision

A campus where lost items are never left behind and every owner can be found.

History of Laguna College

  • Founding & Early Growth (1923): Founded as Laguna Academy by Don Zacarias Sahagun and Don Melecio Fule to provide accessible secondary education for youth in San Pablo City and neighboring towns, growing from an initial 16 students to 1,500 by 1941.
  • Resilience Through World War II: Despite wartime closure during the Japanese occupation and severe damage to its facilities, the institution persevered through the leadership of its alumni and board.
  • Rebirth as Laguna College (1945): Reopened after liberation under its new name, Laguna College, expanding its academic offerings to encompass everything from basic education (kindergarten and elementary) up to college and graduate school.
